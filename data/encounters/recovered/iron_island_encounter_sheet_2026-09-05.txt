POKEMON MERCURY REDUX — IRON ISLAND ENCOUNTER SHEET
Date: 2026-09-05

DESIGN SOURCE
Pokemon Platinum / Sinnoh. Mercury expands the Platinum Iron Island ecology; internal engine identifiers are implementation-only and are not content sources.

IMPLEMENTATION STATUS
STAGED FOR FINAL SINNOH MAPS — The finished player-facing Iron Island maps are not yet constructed in this cumulative source, so these tables are intentionally not attached to unrelated engine geography.

CORE RULES
- Iron Island is an abandoned ore mine / training ground, not a generic cave.
- Each floor has its own encounter identity.
- Platinum anchors (Geodude/Graveler, Zubat/Golbat, Onix, later Steelix) remain recognizable.
- Riolu is NOT a random encounter here. Riley keeps the special Riolu Egg reward; earlier Mercury Riolu availability remains intact.
- While Riley is partnered in B2F, wild battles should become Double Battles and he heals the party after battles.

RILEY / RIOLU
- Riley joins in B2F.
- Wild battles become doubles while he is partnered.
- Riley heals the party after battles.
- After the Galactic encounter, Riley gives a Riolu Egg.
- If the party is full, Mercury should send the Egg to the PC rather than require a Canalave backtrack.
- Riolu remains absent from random Iron Island encounters so the gift stays meaningful.

IRON RUINS
No random wild encounter table. Preserve the Platinum Registeel/ruins concept as special authored content for a later story/static encounter pass.
